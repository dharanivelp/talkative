About Talkative

Talkative is a place to meet new people through one-to-one conversations. Find someone to chat with and get to know each other at your own pace. If you both choose to stay in touch during a conversation, you can keep messaging from your Stayed in touch chats.

Chat with care

  • Treat the person you are chatting with respectfully.
  • Start with a friendly greeting and let the conversation develop at a comfortable pace.
  • Protect your privacy. Do not share your address, phone number, passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information.
  • Harassment and explicit content are not allowed. Use the report or block controls if a conversation makes you uncomfortable.
  • Only agree to stay in touch if you are comfortable doing so. You can remove a saved connection later.

Talkative is for people aged 18 and older. Review our Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy before using the service.
